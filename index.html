ABOUT THE PROJECT

Far From Home, But Never Alone.

LPU Global Connect is a student-support website prototype developed as part of the Far From Home design-thinking project. Its purpose is to make campus information more accessible, simplify everyday communication and help international students feel welcomed and connected.
